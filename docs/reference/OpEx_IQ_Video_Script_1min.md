# OpEx Intelligence Platform — 1-Minute Product Video Script

**Format:** Live UI + motion graphics hybrid  
**Duration:** 60 seconds  
**Pacing:** 140–150 words of voiceover at measured consulting tone  
**Audience:** CFOs, CPOs, and their consulting partners  

---

## SCENE-BY-SCENE BREAKDOWN

---

### 00:00 – 00:06 | HOOK — The Problem

**Visual:**  
Black screen. A single line of white text appears, typewriter style:

> *"Your company spends billions on operations.*  
> *But do you know where it's going — and how much is unnecessary?"*

**On-screen text:** None beyond the quote above.

**Sound:** Low ambient drone, tension building.

**Voiceover (VO):**  
*(None — text does the work. Let it breathe.)*

**On-screen text:**  
`Benchmarked against 100+ listed Indian peers`  
`Source: MCA21 · BSE/NSE · CRISIL · RBI`

`De-duplicated · Confidence-scored · Source-cited`

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Tone
Confident and precise — not hype. This is a CFO audience. Avoid superlatives. Let the numbers do the emoting.

### Music
Recommend: Understated orchestral-tech. Think Hans Zimmer light, not startup SaaS ad. Opens low-tension, resolves to confident. No lyrics.

### Colour palette
Match platform UI: Navy `#1F3864`, Steel Blue `#2E75B6`, White, with Green for positive savings values and Amber for overspend highlights.

### Fonts on screen
Arial or Inter. Bold for numbers. Regular for labels. No decorative typefaces.

### UI screen recordings needed
1. File drag-and-drop into the upload zone (2–3 seconds)
2. Spend treemap populating with animation (3 seconds)
3. Benchmarking bar chart with P50 overlay animating in (5 seconds)
4. Category callout: "78th percentile vs manufacturing peers"
5. Value waterfall chart building left to right (5 seconds)
6. Business case Word document pages flipping (4 seconds)
7. NPV/IRR/Payback summary card close-up (2 seconds)
8. Dashboard hero shot for close (3 seconds)

### Captions / subtitles
Full captions required. Use the clean VO read above verbatim.

### Localization note
All currency shown as INR (₹). If producing a global cut, swap to USD ($) and replace peer data references with "500+ global listed companies."
