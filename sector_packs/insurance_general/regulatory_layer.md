# Regulatory Layer — Insurance General & Life (India)

## Primary Regulators
Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI); SEBI (listed insurers); RBI (bancassurance tie-ups).

## In-Force Regulations with OpEx Impact

### Solvency & Capital
- **IRDAI (Assets, Liabilities and Solvency Margin) Regulations 2016**: Minimum solvency ratio 1.5×; capital charge on investment and underwriting risk — embedded in actuarial and treasury OpEx.
- **Investment Regulations (IRDAI 2016)**: Approved asset classes, exposure limits; compliance monitoring and audit costs.

### Claims & Underwriting
- **Claims Settlement Regulations (IRDAI 2017)**: TAT norms for health/motor claims; grievance redressal infrastructure mandatory.
- **Product Filing & File & Use (IRDAI 2024)**: Faster product approval but ongoing actuarial certification and filing costs per variant.

### Distribution & Conduct
- **Insurance Brokers Regulations 2018 / POSP Guidelines**: Commission caps, training, and KYC for distribution channel; agent network compliance OpEx.
- **Bancassurance Guidelines (IRDAI/RBI)**: Tie-up agreements, incentive caps, and dual-regulator reporting for bank-led distribution.

### Reporting & ESG
- **BRSR Core (SEBI 2023)**: Mandatory FY26 for top-1000 listed insurers; Scope-1/2, water, waste disclosure from operations and branch network.
- **IRDAI Annual Returns & Regulatory Filings**: Statutory returns, appointed actuary report, and reinsurance programme filings.
