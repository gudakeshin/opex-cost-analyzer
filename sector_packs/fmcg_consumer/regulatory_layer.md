# Regulatory Layer — FMCG & Consumer (India)

## Primary Regulators
Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI); Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS); Ministry of Commerce & Industry (MoCI); SEBI (listed FMCG).

## In-Force Regulations with OpEx Impact

### Food & Product Safety
- **FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Act 2006)**: Central/state licence, FSSAI number on packaging, periodic renewal and lab testing OpEx.
- **FSSAI Labelling & Display Regulations 2020**: Nutritional panels, allergen declarations, front-of-pack labelling compliance costs.

### Standards & Quality
- **BIS Act 2016 / QCO notifications**: Mandatory BIS certification for select FMCG categories; testing and hallmarking fees.
- **Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules 2011**: MRP, net quantity, and unit-sale compliance; inspection and penalty risk.

### Tax & Trade
- **GST (CGST Act 2017)**: Rate changes on food, personal care, and packaging inputs; ITC mapping and e-invoice compliance.
- **ASCI Code for Advertising**: Self-regulation on claims; legal review of A&P campaigns.

### Sustainability
- **Plastic Waste Management Rules 2016 (EPR)**: Extended producer responsibility for plastic packaging; buyback and recycling OpEx.
- **BRSR Core (SEBI 2023)**: Mandatory FY26 for listed FMCG; water, waste, and Scope-2 from manufacturing and distribution.
