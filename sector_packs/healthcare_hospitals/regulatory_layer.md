# Regulatory Layer — Healthcare & Hospitals (India)

## Primary Regulators
National Accreditation Board for Hospitals (NABH); MoHFW; CDSCO (pharmacy/drugs); State Health Departments; CPCB (biomedical waste).

## In-Force Regulations with OpEx Impact

### Clinical Quality & Accreditation
- **NABH Standards (5th Edition)**: Accreditation cycle every 4 years; quality indicators, infection control, and documentation OpEx.
- **Clinical Establishments Act (state rules)**: Registration, minimum standards, and periodic inspection costs.

### Pharmacy & Diagnostics
- **Drugs and Cosmetics Act — hospital pharmacy licence**: Cold chain, narcotic registers, and CDSCO compliance for in-house pharmacy.
- **NABL accreditation for labs**: Reagent QC, equipment calibration, and external proficiency testing costs.

### Waste & Environment
- **Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules 2016 (amended 2018)**: Segregation, colour-coded bins, CBWTF contracts, and annual reporting.
- **CPCB norms for healthcare effluent**: ETP operations and stack monitoring for large hospitals.

### Workforce & Safety
- **Nursing Council / Clinical Staff norms**: Nurse-patient ratios, CME, and credentialing costs embedded in clinical_staff OpEx.
- **BRSR Core (SEBI 2023)**: Mandatory FY26 for listed hospital chains; energy, water, and waste from facilities.
