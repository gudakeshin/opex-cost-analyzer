# Regulatory Layer — Organised Retail (India)

## Primary Regulators
Ministry of Commerce & Industry (MoCI); FSSAI (food retail); SEBI (listed retailers); CCI (competition); state Shops & Establishments departments.

## In-Force Regulations with OpEx Impact

### Store Operations & Labour
- **Shops & Establishments Acts (state)**: Working hours, weekly off, registers, and inspection compliance across store network.
- **Model Shops & Establishment Act 2016**: Night operations, women workforce norms; HR and scheduling OpEx.

### Food & Consumer Safety
- **FSSAI (retail food handlers)**: Licence per store/kitchen; hygiene audits for in-store bakeries and fresh food counters.
- **Legal Metrology (retail MRP)**: Weighing scales calibration, packaged goods labelling at point of sale.

### Tax & Invoicing
- **GST e-invoicing / e-way bill**: Mandatory above turnover thresholds; POS and ERP integration compliance OpEx.
- **TCS on e-commerce (Sec 194-O)**: Marketplace collection obligations for omnichannel retailers.

### Real Estate & Competition
- **Model Tenancy Act / state rent control**: Lease registration, CAM reconciliation, and occupancy cost governance.
- **CCI vertical/horizontal agreements**: Vendor tie-ups, exclusive arrangements; legal review of merchandising contracts.
- **BRSR Core (SEBI 2023)**: Mandatory FY26 for listed retailers; store energy, shrinkage reporting, supply chain ESG.
