# Regulatory Layer — Financial Services Non-Bank (India)

## Primary Regulators
Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI); Reserve Bank of India (NBFC/MFI directions); Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI); PFRDA (pension funds).

## In-Force Regulations with OpEx Impact

### Capital Markets & Asset Management
- **SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations 1996**: Scheme filing, TER caps, distributor due diligence; fund admin and compliance OpEx.
- **SEBI LODR (2015)**: Disclosure, related-party, and governance for listed NBFCs and AMC parents.

### Lending & NBFC
- **RBI Master Direction — NBFC Scale-Based Regulation (2023)**: Layered capital, governance, and disclosure by NBFC layer.
- **RBI Fair Practices Code / Outsourcing Directions**: Collection agency oversight, third-party risk, and audit costs.

### KYC & Investor Protection
- **CKYC / KYC Master Directions (RBI/SEBI)**: Central KYC registry, re-KYC cycles, and AML transaction monitoring systems.
- **PMLA 2002 / FATF alignment**: SAR filing, designated director, and annual AML audit OpEx.

### Pension & Insurance Distribution
- **PFRDA (Pension Fund) Regulations**: NPS scheme compliance, custodian fees, and actuarial reporting.
- **IRDAI bancassurance / corporate agent norms**: Where NBFCs distribute insurance — dual-regulator compliance.
