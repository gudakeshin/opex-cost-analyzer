# Regulatory Layer — Pharma & Life Sciences (India)

## Primary Regulators
Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO); USFDA / EMA (export markets); Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (MoHFW); DPIIT (PLI schemes).

## In-Force Regulations with OpEx Impact

### Manufacturing & Quality
- **Drugs and Cosmetics Act 1940 / Schedule M (GMP)**: Facility qualification, batch records, stability studies; validation and QC lab OpEx.
- **WHO-GMP / PIC/S alignment (CDSCO 2018+)**: Harmonised GMP inspections; remediation costs on 483/observation letters.

### Product Registration & Filings
- **New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules 2019**: CT approvals, ethics committee, pharmacovigilance system mandatory.
- **ANDA/NDA / DMF filings (USFDA/CDSCO)**: Regulatory affairs, CRO fees, and bioequivalence study costs per product.

### Pharmacovigilance & Safety
- **Pharmacovigilance Programme of India (PvPI)**: ADR reporting, signal detection, and periodic safety update reports (PSURs).
- **USFDA 21 CFR Part 11 / EU GMP Annex 11**: Electronic records and validated IT systems for regulated manufacturing.

### Incentives & ESG
- **PLI Scheme for Pharma (DoP notifications)**: Incremental sales thresholds; annual compliance certification and audit OpEx.
- **BRSR Core (SEBI 2023)**: Mandatory FY26 for listed pharma; environmental and social metrics from API/plant operations.
